# Post-run evaluation

Evaluate only after the session. Scores are diagnostic, not optimization targets.

Rate each dimension from 0 to 3 and support the rating with trail evidence.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Information-environment breadth | One environment | Minor source variation | Several distinct environments | Multiple environments with justified crossovers |
| Path coherence | Hops are unexplained | Some causal bridges | Most hops are traceable | Every major transition is materially justified |
| Semantic displacement | Ending repeats the seed | Small reframing | Clear shift in objects or questions | Destination could not reasonably be predicted from the seed |
| Question emergence | No new question | Reworded initial question | A materially different question appears | The initial frame is abandoned for a stronger unforeseen question |
| Explanation revision | Initial interpretation survives untouched | Cosmetic change | One important revision | Several concrete facts force structural revision |
| Joint discovery gain | Human+AI adds nothing | Faster only | Finds material one arm missed | Finds a route both human-only and AI-only missed |
| Provenance coverage | Sources missing | Partial titles or URLs | Most important claims traceable | Observations, inferences and uncertainty are consistently separated |
| Cross-run repetition | Same route every run | Mostly repeated | Meaningful variation | Different but coherent routes emerge from the same seed |

## Recommended comparison

Use the same seed, budget and tool access for three independent arms:

1. human only;
2. AI only;
3. human + AI using CoExplore.

Compare route overlap, unique sources, question changes and intervention effects. Do not reveal one arm's trail to another until all runs end.

## Caveat

Semantic distance and novelty are not inherently valuable. A far destination without a defensible trail is noise; an unusual source that changes nothing is novelty theater.
