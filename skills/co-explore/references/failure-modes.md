# Failure Modes

These are recurring problems observed in real human–AI exploration. They are reminders to keep exploration from collapsing back into familiar paths.

## Premature convergence
A plausible explanation appears and later browsing becomes evidence collection for it. Fix: keep acquiring concrete material the explanation cannot comfortably contain before naming a topic.

## Semantic attractors
The trail repeatedly returns to the same AI, technology, news, paper, or familiar-industry ecology. Fix: change platform, era, community, language, or source type — not merely keywords.

## Source monoculture
Many URLs belong to the same information ecosystem. Fix: when material becomes homogeneous, cross into a different information environment.

## Explanation replacing discovery
No new material is acquired while the prose becomes increasingly “deep.” Fix: stop commentary and acquire a new source, object, or fact.

## Goal smuggling
The run claims to be open-ended while secretly optimizing for a good topic, business opportunity, or conclusion. Fix: allow the run to produce nothing and navigate by material rather than deliverables.

## Mid-run summary contamination
A polished summary becomes the new attractor and later navigation starts serving it. Fix: avoid synthesis while moving; if orientation is necessary, preserve only raw facts, dead ends, and live forks, then continue immediately.

## Fake randomness
In real parallel runs, independent agents both chose Wikipedia as a supposedly random starting point and later drifted toward similar substitute “random sites.” Fix: do not treat a random-site picker as exploration. After the seed, moves should be caused by material while crossing information environments when needed.

## Protocolized wandering
Fixed step counts, scheduled reviews, progress indicators, or required intermediate outputs make the model serve the process instead of the material. An early CoExplore version produced exactly this failure: even when the user asked for continuous wandering, the Skill pulled behavior toward quotas and review points. Fix: no fixed pacing; keep moving while material is alive, reroute when a branch dries up, and let user intent plus exploration state determine when the run ends.

## Mechanical opposition
“Break the bubble” becomes “find the opposite political viewpoint.” Fix: leave the entire frame when useful instead of merely switching sides on the same axis.

## Fully autonomous performance
The AI browses many pages but human surprise, boredom, rejection, or curiosity cannot change the route. Fix: treat “too familiar / dull / follow this detail” as genuine navigation signals.

## Permission at every step
The opposite failure is stopping before every link to ask whether to continue. Fix: the AI should continue autonomously along live clues and expose forks only when human judgment would materially change the route; the user can interrupt at any time.
