# Failure modes

| Failure mode | Observable signal | Correction |
|---|---|---|
| Premature convergence | A topic or thesis appears after one or two interesting items | Ban synthesis for the next segment and collect materially different sources |
| Semantic attractor | Every hop remains a close synonym of the previous query | Follow an object, institution, person, practice or historical predecessor instead of another keyword |
| Source monoculture | Many URLs, one platform or source type | Move to a different information environment using a concrete bridge from current material |
| Explanation substitution | The agent produces deeper commentary without new evidence | Stop writing interpretation; acquire and record new material |
| Objective smuggling | The route quietly optimizes for a publishable topic, business opportunity or preferred belief | Restate non-goals and allow the current framing to be discarded |
| Summary contamination | A checkpoint's wording becomes the vocabulary of every later search | Return to raw observations and pick a detail not represented in the summary |
| Fake randomness | The agent uses a random page but then follows familiar defaults | Require a disclosed seed method and causal bridge for every hop |
| Contrarian trap | “Breaking the bubble” becomes merely finding the opposite opinion | Leave the binary frame and explore different communities, objects or questions |
| Agent theater | Human input is reduced to approval while AI autonomously repeats itself | Ask for anomaly signals and make human interventions alter the route |
| Path laundering | The final answer hides failed branches and makes the route look inevitable | Preserve the trail and state why branches were abandoned |
| Novelty theater | Exotic sources are collected without changing understanding | Test whether a concrete question, explanation or route actually changed |
| Infinite wandering | No stop condition and no useful trail review | Use a declared budget and end with a retrospective, not forced closure |
