# Contributing

CoExplore needs evidence from real exploration more than prettier wording.

Useful contributions include:

- anonymized real exploration trails;
- concrete failure cases such as premature convergence, source monoculture, and fake randomness;
- cross-model comparisons from the same seed;
- new language versions;
- improvements that help human and AI genuinely leave familiar information paths.

Do not commit credentials, private browsing history, sensitive personal information, or material you do not have the right to publish.

For real exploration contributions, please include the starting point, important human interventions, how the route changed, unexpected discoveries, and what actually changed by the end.
